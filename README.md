# 🛒 Customer Behavior Analysis

![Dashboard Preview](https://github.com/snehez/CustomerBehaviorAnalysis/blob/main/Dashboard.png)

## 📌 Introduction
This project analyzes customer purchasing behavior using a combination of **Python (Google Colab)**, **SQL (MySQL Workbench)**, and **Power BI dashboards**.  

The dataset captures details about purchases, customer demographics, payment methods, returns, and churn.  

The objective is to extract **financially strong, business-oriented insights** relevant to the banking & retail sectors (e.g., ICICI Bank, HDFC Bank), focusing on:

- Customer segmentation  
- Revenue optimization  
- Churn prevention  
- Payment preferences  
- Return behavior  

---

## ⚙️ Tools & Technologies
- **Python (Google Colab):** Data cleaning, visualization (Matplotlib, Seaborn, Pandas)  
- **MySQL Workbench:** Querying customer insights using SQL  
- **Power BI:** Interactive dashboard for executives  
- **CSV Data:** Raw dataset of customer transactions  

---

## 📊 Python Analysis 
**Key Visualizations & Insights:**
- **Top Product Categories:** Home & Electronics drive the highest revenue  
- **Monthly Revenue Trend:** Sales spike during festive months (Oct–Dec)  
- **Payment Method Analysis:** Credit Card is the most preferred, followed by PayPal  
- **Age Group Analysis:** Young Adults (25–40) contribute the most to purchases  
- **Churn Analysis:** High-value customers with declining activity show churn risk  

**📌 Recommendation:**  
Launch loyalty programs for high-value customers, EMI offers on Credit Cards, and targeted promotions during festive seasons.

---

## 🗄️ SQL Analysis

**Key Business Questions Solved:**
1. Who are the top spenders (VIP customers)?  
2. Which product categories generate the most revenue?  
3. Which payment methods are most used?  
4. What is the return rate per category?  
5. Which age groups drive the most sales?  
6. Do men or women spend more on average?  
7. What are the monthly revenue trends?  
8. Which customers are most likely to churn?  
9. Which product categories are popular among young customers (<25)?  
10. Who are the top 5 customers with the most returns?  

**📌 Recommendation:**  
Focus on reducing returns in Electronics, upsell to young customers, and monitor high churn-risk segments.

## 📊 Power BI Dashboard

**📌 Recommendation:**  
Use the dashboard for **real-time monitoring of KPIs** and executive decision-making.

---

## ✅ Insights & Recommendations

- **Revenue Drivers:** Home & Electronics categories are critical  
- **High-Value Customers:** A small percentage of customers drive a large portion of revenue → personalize offers  
- **Churn Prevention:** Customers with fewer transactions in recent months need retention strategies  
- **Returns Management:** Electronics have high returns → improve product quality or after-sales service  
- **Banking Synergy:** Credit Card spending dominance shows opportunity for EMI & cashback offers  

---

## ⚠️ Shortcomings & Limitations

- Dataset is limited → may not cover all customer demographics  
- Return & churn data is historical, not predictive  
- Does not include marketing spend or profitability data  
- Power BI dashboard is static without real-time pipeline
